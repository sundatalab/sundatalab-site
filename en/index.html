04

Compliance isn't optional

Since August 2, 2026, a first set of obligations under the European AI Act applies to businesses, with penalties of up to €35 million or 7% of worldwide revenue. More than one SME in two still doesn't know precisely what it's allowed to do, and many are already using AI tools that aren't tracked internally.

Whether you license Isalis or we build your custom-built tool, GDPR & AI Act compliance is a condition of doing this seriously, not a box to tick afterward. We also offer a dedicated assessment for businesses that first want to know where they stand.
