Sundatalab's AI Act diagnostic: what it covers, and its price

Sundatalab's AI Act diagnostic costs between €5,000 and €10,000, over 2 to 3 weeks. Unlike a free self-assessment with a handful of questions, it covers your company's actual systems: here is precisely what it assesses, and why it is billed.
Request a diagnostic See compliance in detail
01

Why it isn't free

Several online tools offer a free AI Act self-assessment, in a three-to-five-minute questionnaire. Sundatalab made a different choice: a few minutes of questions cannot capture a company's actual usage, in particular the usage management itself is unaware of.

Billing for the diagnostic guarantees it is carried through to the end, by our teams, on the systems actually in use. It's the same principle as the Studio's audit: our diagnostics sometimes conclude there is nothing to build next, and that is precisely what makes them credible.

02

Once the register is in place, two independent next steps are possible: ongoing regulatory monitoring (€300 to €800 per month) or strategic guidance, quoted separately. See the detail of all three services.

03

Why do it now

The obligation to train staff on AI and the ban on certain unacceptable-risk practices have been in force since February 2025. Since August 2, 2026, a new set of obligations applies, notably on the transparency of AI systems and their risk classification.

Sanctions can reach €35 million or 7% of global revenue. An up-to-date register, produced by the diagnostic, is what protects the company before an audit becomes a problem.

04

Frequently asked questions

Does the diagnostic really cost between €5,000 and €10,000?

Yes: that's the range Sundatalab applies for a complete diagnostic. The exact amount within that range depends on company size and the number of AI systems to inventory; it is confirmed before the engagement starts, never after.

Why don't you offer a free diagnostic, like other online tools?

Because a few minutes of questions cannot capture a company's actual usage, especially usage that management itself isn't aware of. Billing for the diagnostic guarantees it is carried through to the end, including when the conclusion is that there is nothing to build next.

What exactly does the diagnostic include?

An inventory of every AI system used in the company, including undeclared usage, its risk-level classification, a ready-to-use compliance register, and a prioritized action plan going forward.

How long does the diagnostic take?

2 to 3 weeks, from the first conversation to delivery of the register and the action plan.

Are we required to continue with regulatory monitoring after the diagnostic?

No. The diagnostic is a standalone service, billed separately. Regulatory monitoring (€300 to €800 per month) and strategic guidance are independent options, to activate only if you wish.
