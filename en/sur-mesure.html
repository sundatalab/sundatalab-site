01

Why custom-built, and why now

81% of SMEs that invest in AI choose a custom-built solution or bespoke development rather than a standard tool (Bpifrance, Baromètre IA 2026). This isn't an aesthetic preference: a standard tool is designed for the average business, and yours isn't.

At the same time, 60% of SMEs that want to build AI skills in-house don't succeed (Bpifrance, Baromètre IA 2026). Hiring is slow, expensive, and one person never covers building, running and compliance all at once. Sundatalab's custom-built approach answers both: a tool that fits your business, without you having to hire to get it.

05

Why the audit is billed

A business owner has often already received several "free audits" that were, in reality, just disguised sales pitches. Charging for the audit, and delivering a document that's genuinely usable (including if it concludes that nothing should be built), is what makes the approach credible.

It's also a matter of rigor: our tools run in front of brands that tolerate no deviation on their image. The standard is the same regardless of your company's size.

06

Frequently asked questions

Am I committed to building after the audit?

No. The audit is a standalone service, billed separately. You then decide, freely, whether or not to launch the build.

Will the tool be integrated with our existing systems?

Yes, that's the purpose of the build phase: the tool is designed to integrate with your current systems, not to force you to change them.

What happens if our need changes after delivery?

That's the role of optional maintenance: we adjust the tool as your business changes, without starting a new project every time.

Do you work with sensitive or regulated data?

Yes, in compliance with GDPR and the AI Act. See our compliance support for the details of what this involves.
