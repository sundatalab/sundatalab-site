Our story

SMEs and small businesses keep the economy running. It's time technology worked for them too.

Sundatalab was founded on a conviction: those who drive the economy every day deserve the same tools as large corporations, in their full version, operational within weeks.
01

With Sundatalab, your business benefits from advances in artificial intelligence at the same pace as the largest corporations

Every wave of technology benefits the same companies first. They have the team, the budget, the IT department to absorb change first. The rest watch, adapt when they can, and pay more when they finally get there.

With artificial intelligence, we chose to reverse this logic from day one. Nearly one in two SMEs has already launched an AI project: the will is there. Our role is to put tools designed for their business into their hands, on equal footing with those built for large corporations.

02

A thirty-person company gets access to the same AI power as a company of three thousand, thanks to a method designed for it

This is the conviction on which Sundatalab was founded. Not a slogan: a principle that guided the first tool we built, for ourselves. Isalis was not born from a product plan in a meeting room. It was born from a real need, the day producing brand content by hand was no longer sustainable.

We built it for ourselves. Then other executives, facing the same wall, asked us for the same thing for their business. It became our reason for being.

03

SMEs and small businesses are not a secondary market. They are the engine of the economy.

They deserve tools worthy of that role: not the leftovers of innovation, once large corporations are done with it. Helping a company of twenty, fifty, or a hundred people grow through AI means helping the real economy move forward, not just adding another line to a product catalogue.

Democratizing AI is not about making it simpler. It's about making it fair.

Purpose

Making sure artificial intelligence also benefits those who don't have the resources of large corporations.

AI has created a new divide: on one side, companies with teams, budgets, and time; on the other, the vast majority. This divide is a matter of access, cost, and expertise, not technical inevitability.

Vision

That a thirty-person company uses AI as naturally, and as effectively, as a company of three thousand.

Achieved the day an executive no longer wonders whether they can afford AI, but which tool solves their problem, just as they would for accounting or telephony.

Mission

Designing, building, and running AI tools that help companies grow.

Ours, available under license. Theirs, custom-built.
