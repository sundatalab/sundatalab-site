Frequently asked questions

Is Isalis built by another company, or by Sundatalab?

Isalis is designed, developed and used in-house by Sundatalab before being licensed to our clients. It is not a third-party tool we resell.

How much does Isalis cost?

Isalis is available as a license, from €19 to €199 per month depending on volume, brands and channels. Full plan details and a trial are on isalis.ai.

Does Isalis publish content automatically, without human review?

No. Isalis learns your brand's visual identity and tone, then creates your visuals, videos and copy, but nothing is published to your social channels without your prior approval.

Is Isalis GDPR and AI Act compliant?

Yes. Unlike many free AI tools from large US platforms, which are not currently available in Europe for lack of compliance, Isalis is designed and hosted for Europe from day one.

Where can I find Isalis's full features, use cases and pricing?

On isalis.ai, the product's dedicated site. This page presents Isalis in the context of Sundatalab, the company that built it and uses it every day.
