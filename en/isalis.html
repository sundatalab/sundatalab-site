01

What Isalis does for you

Isalis is a tool for creating and publishing brand content. It learns your visual identity, tone and codes from what you show it (brand guidelines, sample posts, brand documents), then produces visuals, short videos and copy tailored to each channel.

Every piece of content goes through human validation before it's published: you keep control over what goes out under your name. Isalis then schedules publication across your channels, at the times you choose. It's the first tool we built, for ourselves, before offering it to our clients. We run it in-house, every day.

Built for Europe, from day one

Many free AI tools from major US platforms are still not available in Europe, because they haven't come into compliance with the GDPR and the AI Act. Isalis, by contrast, has been hosted and designed for Europe from the start.

This isn't an option you switch on later: it's a condition for using the tool without legal risk, both today and once new European requirements come into force.

07

Frequently asked questions

How long before the first posts go live?

A few days are enough for Isalis to learn your brand identity once your materials are uploaded. The first content proposals arrive shortly after, ready for your approval before publishing.

Do I have to approve everything before publishing?

Yes, by default. It's a core principle of the product, not a hidden setting: no content goes out under your name without your explicit approval.

Can I change plans along the way?

Yes. The subscription is monthly with no long-term commitment: you can adjust your plan as your needs evolve.

Does Isalis replace an agency or a dedicated communications person?

Isalis replaces the production time you don't have, not human judgment. You remain the decision-maker on what gets published; the tool produces and schedules.

What if my needs go beyond Isalis?

If your needs are specific to your business and don't fit a standard tool, that's the role of custom development, not Isalis.
